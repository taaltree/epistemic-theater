Lexicon

No. 04012:51
Logical asymmetry
No finite set of confirming instances proves a universal claim, but one genuine counterinstance refutes it.
No. 04113:03
Corroboration
Popper’s term for a theory’s record of surviving severe attempts at refutation; not proof or probability.
No. 04213:21
Falsifiability
The property of a theory that some possible observation would contradict it; Popper’s mark of science.
No. 04313:32
Nonconsumptive effects
Effects of predators on prey through fear: changes in behavior or physiology that may reduce fitness without killing.
No. 04414:04
Auxiliary hypothesis
An assumption, such as how an instrument or index works, needed alongside the main hypothesis to derive a prediction.
No. 04514:24
Duhem–Quine thesis
Hypotheses are tested only in conjunction with auxiliary assumptions, so a failed prediction cannot by itself identify what is false.
No. 04614:56
Research program
Lakatos: a hard core of central commitments, shielded by a protective belt of revisable auxiliary hypotheses.
No. 04714:56

A program is progressive when its adjustments predict novel facts that are confirmed, degenerating when they only accommodate failures.
No. 04815:29

Testing whether data are improbable under a null model, usually of no effect; rejecting it does not test a substantive hypothesis severely.

Counterfactual

Rehearsal answers

What separates induction from abduction, and where does strong inference fit?

Induction generalizes from observed cases to unobserved ones; abduction infers the hypothesis that would best explain a surprising fact. Strong inference disciplines abduction by generating rival explanations and designing tests that exclude some, so the loveliest story must also survive elimination.

Why does the Duhem–Quine thesis complicate falsification?

A prediction follows from a hypothesis plus auxiliary assumptions, so a failed prediction refutes only the conjunction. Logic cannot say whether the hypothesis or an auxiliary (an index, an instrument, a measure of risk) is at fault; Lakatos asks whether rescuing adjustments predict novel facts.

What did Kuhn mean by incommensurability?

Rival paradigms differ in problems, standards, and meanings of terms, so there is no neutral measure by which a single test decides between them; choice relies on shared values weighed by judgment.

Why is compensatory mortality a counterfactual claim?

It asserts that animals killed by a predator would have died anyway from other causes: a claim about an unobserved outcome. Evidence such as the age and condition of killed animals (wolf-killed cow elk ~16 years, hunter-killed ~4) bears on it indirectly.

What is the argument from inductive risk?

Because evidence never makes a hypothesis certain, accepting it requires judging the evidence sufficient, and sufficiency depends on the costs of false acceptance and false rejection. Non-epistemic values therefore legitimately set evidential thresholds (Rudner; Douglas), though they must not replace evidence.

How does the answer to “what counts as science?” change with the criterion?

Under falsifiability even astrology qualifies; under institutional norms and progress interpretive research qualifies and astrology does not; under cluster, systematicity, or Wissenschaft accounts both research traditions qualify. The practices do not change; the label does.
